[2000] RESOLUTIONS IN PROGRESS
==============================

LAST UPDATED
------------
2026-10-15

PURPOSE
-------
This document is the top-level owner for the Resolutions In Progress series,
defining its scope and organisation.

The Resolutions In Progress series contains topics that are not yet fully
settled and require ongoing assessment, investigation, validation, or
decision-making.

SYSTEM GOVERNANCE
-----------------
The Resolutions In Progress series is part of the wider knowledge-base system.

Ownership boundaries, hierarchy relationships, and evolution are defined in:

    [0000] REGISTRY OVERVIEW

CONTENT RULE
------------
Information in the Resolutions In Progress series will evolve over time.

The series includes:

- current project status
- current project position
- open questions
- unresolved decisions
- active investigations
- pending validation
- known uncertainties

The series does not normally contain:

- established knowledge
- enduring reference information
- active execution activities
- action lists
- supportive evidence
- forensic analysis

SERIES CONTENTS
---------------

[2100] CURRENT PROJECT STATE

    Current project position, status, metrics, and progress indicators.

[2200] OPEN QUESTIONS

    Outstanding questions, unresolved decisions, and investigations.

RESOLUTIONS IN PROGRESS RULE
----------------------------
Information should remain within the Resolutions In Progress series while
significant uncertainty, investigation, or decisions remain ongoing.

Resolution may involve:

- gathering additional evidence
- performing validation activities
- answering open questions
- resolving contradictions
- reaching project decisions

Only information considered sufficiently understood, validated and stable
should become part of the Knowledge Established series.
