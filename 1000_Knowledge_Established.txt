[1000] KNOWLEDGE ESTABLISHED
============================

LAST UPDATED
------------
2026-10-15

PURPOSE
-------
This document is the top-level owner for the Knowledge Established series,
defining its scope and organisation.

The Knowledge Established series contains information that is considered
sufficiently understood, validated, and stable to form part of the established
body of project knowledge.

SYSTEM GOVERNANCE
-----------------
The Knowledge Established series is part of the wider knowledge-base system.

Ownership boundaries, hierarchy relationships, and evolution are defined in:

    [0000] REGISTRY OVERVIEW

CONTENT RULE
------------
Information in the Knowledge Established series should be relatively stable.

The series includes:

- principles
- context
- reference information
- validated findings
- established decisions
- enduring project knowledge

The series does not normally contain:

- unresolved questions
- active investigations
- current execution activities
- action lists
- supportive evidence
- forensic analysis

SERIES CONTENTS
---------------

[1100] PRINCIPLES AND CONTEXT

    Principles, context, rationale, and enduring project understanding.

[1200] REFERENCE INFORMATION

    Reference material, systems, environments, and lookup information.

[1300] VALIDATED FINDINGS

    Proven conclusions that should not normally require reinvestigation.

KNOWLEDGE ESTABLISHED RULE
--------------------------
Information should only be promoted into the Knowledge Established series when
it is considered sufficiently understood and supported.

Where significant uncertainty remains, the information should remain outside
the Knowledge Established series until sufficient confidence has been achieved.

Only information considered stable and established should become part of the
Knowledge Established series.
